Leadership

I haven't taken part in many leadership roles during school but whenever I get a chance, such as a group project, I try and take charge. Through many of these oppurtunites I was able to make small tweaks and changes to further develop this skill.

For example, delegating tasks to group members and ensuring everyone is heard when making decisions and equally sharing the workload.

Communication

This is a skill that I am still currently building upon as I am not as confident in it, but still very proud of how far it has come along.

To continue improving it in the future, I plan to practice it more during group projects, presentations and collaborative work in general. Through these group activities, I hope to further my confidence in talking to others, both new and familiar people.

Time Management

I have recently improved this skill a lot, especially since I had to take schoolwork more seriously coming into year 11 at OSC.

I usually practice this by planning out my I-time, attending tutorials when needed and allocating time at home to make progress and complete unfinished work from class.

Additionally, I set goals in my planner and write reminders of what to do to keep things organised and easy to begin which makes me more motivated to start and continue with work for a longer period of time.

Reliability

All throughout OJC my teachers found me especially trustworthy and dependable, giving me the opportunity to take on more responsibilities whenever it they came up.

For example, my MAC/LA teacher would give me the job of creating a goodbye card with pictures and signnituares from the whole class when someone was leaving our MAC.

I would work on it throughout the day and make sure it was done on time and signed by everyone to keep it a suprise when we had MAC time. I did this multiple times throughout the year and became a trusted, reliable student among my peers and teachers.

Work Ethic

Over the years, I have slowly developed a strong work ethic at school. I have learned to take time with assignments and complete them to the best of my ability even if it takes longer than expected.

As a result, I have noticed that I am more proud to show off my hard work and spend more time learning to finish it to the best degree.

Perseverance

During my semester one class, Photography, I was assigned new learning guides every week while learning editing techniques, photography concepts and how to use the camera which I found a bit difficult to keep up with at times.

The Tuakiri Unit was a project I was working on nearing the end of the semester. It included many photos, editing, layout planning and study of different artist models.

Due to the complexity of the project, I initially struggled to keep up with the workload. However, I took inititave and spent my own time during the weekends to travel away from hom and out into the city and take more pictures. As a result, I was more proud than ever to showcase my work to friends and family.
